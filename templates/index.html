02 · Interpretability

What drives the prediction

Permutation importance from the ensemble, normalized across 23 engineered features. Nothing here is a black box — every prediction comes with a feature-level explanation.

Key finding

Attendance is the single strongest signal — stronger than prior grades. A student at 95% attendance with average study habits typically outperforms a high-achiever at 70%.

Fairness

Protected attributes are excluded from the feature set. Disparate-impact ratio across subgroups: 0.97 (threshold ≥ 0.80).

Explainability

Every prediction ships with SHAP-style contribution bars, so advisors can see why, not just what.
